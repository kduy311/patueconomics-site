Research

My research advances two complementary streams at the intersection of international strategy and entrepreneurship in emerging and transition economies, anchored empirically in Vietnam and the Mekong Delta.

Research statement

1 · Internationalization & foreign direct investment

I investigate FDI entry-mode decisions, the push–pull location determinants of foreign direct investment, and the internationalization–firm-performance relationship in emerging and transition economies, drawing on international-strategy theory and empirical evidence across Vietnam, ASEAN, and a range of comparator markets.

2 · Entrepreneurship under weak institutions

A sustained program on bribery, corruption, and informal-economy dynamics, examining which entrepreneurs bribe and what they gain, how institutional quality and national-culture distance shape behavior, and how entrepreneurial intention and value creation emerge under institutional constraint.

Related work addresses institutional quality, national-culture distance and bilateral trade (gravity models), and SME competitiveness and regional linkage in the Mekong Delta.

Both streams are published in journals including Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice, European Management Review, and the Asian Academy of Management Journal.
